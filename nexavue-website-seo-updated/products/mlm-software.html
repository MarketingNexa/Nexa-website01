Grow Your Multi Level Marketing Business

Multi level marketing (MLM) success depends on accurate commission tracking and a transparent downline structure. NexaVue Technologies delivers powerful multi level marketing software that automates member onboarding, genealogy management, commission calculation and payout processing.

Whether you operate a Binary Plan, Matrix Plan, Unilevel or hybrid compensation model, our MLM software adapts to your network marketing strategy while keeping every distributor informed with real-time dashboards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Multi Level Marketing software?

Multi Level Marketing software is a platform that manages distributors, downline genealogy, commission calculation and payouts for MLM and direct selling companies.

Which MLM compensation plans do you support?

NexaVue supports Binary, Matrix, Unilevel, Board, Generation and hybrid multi level marketing plans, all fully configurable.

Is the MLM software secure?

Yes. Our multi level marketing software uses role-based access, encrypted data and audited commission logs to keep member and payout data safe.
